About the engine

What it is made of

  • An HTML5 tokenizer and tree builder, checked against the html5lib tests.
  • A CSS parser with selectors, the cascade and computed styles.
  • Block and inline layout with line breaking, including Japanese text.
  • A display list painted by Vulkan on the GPU, with a CPU renderer that the tests compare it against.
  • A JavaScript engine: a parser for the whole of ES2024 and a bytecode compiler and interpreter shared with the coming WebAssembly engine.

What comes next

  • The built-in objects of JavaScript, then scripts in pages and the DOM.
  • Floats, positioning, flexbox and grid.
  • HTTP and HTTPS, images (PNG, JPEG, GIF) and WebAssembly.
