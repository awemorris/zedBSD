# Keiland artwork

`zedbsd-boot-splash.png` is the boot screen the glass mark, the word
"zedBSD", "operating system", a dotted spinner, over a bright blurred
lake landscape.

Fonts do not reach a picture made with them. It is the design base for
the boot screen, the wallpaper, the greeter, the lock screen and the
file manager's Welcome. Other reference pictures stay out of the tree.

The mark (mark.c) takes its motif from the sacred stone rod and stone
boat of the Jomon period at the Togariishi site, held as the gods of
the descendants' prosperity.
