## Notes

- **zedbsd**: The zlib license asks for no notice in binaries; the image carries the project's license (the zedbsd-license package, always selected) as the list's first entry.
- **musl-regex**: src/libc/regex/LICENSE holds TRE's BSD-2-Clause and musl's MIT texts; the zedbsd-license package installs it, as libc is in every image.
- **i915-intel**: userland/base/licenses/i915-driver/LICENSE (the copyright notices of the derived parts and the MIT permission notice), installed by i915-driver-license, selected with CONFIG_DRIVER_PCI_I915.
- **khronos-headers**: Each header carries its own license text, which travels with it into the development root filesystem.
- **noct**: Zlib asks for no notice in binaries. The clang resource headers in its tree are not installed.
- **emacs**: 2026-10-04 user: taken into userland/base/emacs and licensed by its author under zlib; no special treatment (D1 closed).
- **ime-dict-ja**: Under the project's license file (ws095 D1).
- **ime-dict-skk**: Under the project's license file (ws095 D1); no entry is copied from SKK-JISYO.L.
- **hand-hershey**: The Unicode mapping of the glyphs taken is from Kaloffl's Hershey-Unicode-Mapping (Unlicense).
- **intelbt-firmware**: Unmodified binaries only; the licence forbids reverse engineering.
- **ffmpeg**: LGPL 2.1 or later as built (no --enable-gpl, --enable-version3 or --enable-nonfree; configure must report it). Shared libraries, so they can be replaced. In the image by the user's decision of 2026-10-05 (WS122: libavcodec and a simple player in beta 1); the corresponding source is the upstream tarball with no patches and the configure options in the package Makefile.
